About Me

Hello! My name is Di Jiaqi (狄加齐). I am a graduate student majoring in Control Engineering at Northeastern University, China.

My academic interests include control systems, semiconductor thermal management, electronic device reliability, and engineering optimization.

I have completed internships at Google and Microsoft, gaining valuable exposure to engineering practices and technology-driven innovation.

My research interests also involve heat dissipation challenges in next-generation foldable electronic devices, particularly transistor-level thermal behavior and device reliability.

I enjoy exploring complex engineering problems and developing practical solutions through research and innovation.
